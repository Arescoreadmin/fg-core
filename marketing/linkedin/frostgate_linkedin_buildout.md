## Claim Safety

### Safe to Claim Publicly

These are supported by repo materials and product screens:

- FrostGate is an AI governance and evidence control plane.
- FrostGate supports AI governance assessments.
- FrostGate maps work to frameworks including NIST AI RMF, SOC 2, HIPAA, CMMC, and ISO 27001.
- FrostGate includes connector-driven evidence collection across Microsoft 365, OAuth risk, Entra governance, SharePoint and OneDrive, endpoint inventory, DNS/email, web headers, and network posture.
- FrostGate supports signed/versioned reports, evidence lineage, remediation roadmaps, client portal delivery, audit trails, and tenant-scoped workflows.
- FrostGate is built around evidence integrity, deterministic reporting, policy gating, and audit readiness.

### Use Carefully

Use these only with context:

- "Continuous compliance" - best phrased as "continuous compliance workflows" or "continuous governance operations" unless production verification is complete for the specific customer use case.
- "Autonomous governance" - good for roadmap or enterprise layer, but avoid implying unsupervised production autonomy.
- "Audit-ready" - acceptable as "audit-ready evidence" or "audit-ready exports"; avoid saying "certified" or "guaranteed compliant."
- "Regulated industries" - good as target market, but do not imply regulatory approval.

### Do Not Claim Yet

Avoid unless separately verified before posting:

- Certified HIPAA, SOC 2, CMMC, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, or DoD/IL5 compliance.
- Guaranteed compliance outcomes.
- Fully autonomous remediation in production.
- Air-gapped or offline verification unless the exact capability is production-ready and documented.
- Customer counts, revenue, named clients, or production pilot results unless you have permission and evidence.
