# FrostGate AI Governance Field Assessment
## One-Page Overview

---

**What FrostGate does**

FrostGate runs a structured field assessment of how your organization is using artificial intelligence tools — and what risks that use introduces. Using 13 automated connectors and a 69-control governance questionnaire, we evaluate your AI tool inventory, identity and access posture, data exposure, endpoint compliance, email and DNS security, and alignment with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. The result is a written report with specific findings, a risk score, and a prioritized remediation roadmap delivered in a secure portal — the same kind of assessment that larger organizations pay tens of thousands of dollars for, completed in a single working session.

---

**What you get**

- **Secure portal access** — a private, password-protected portal at app.frostgate.ai with your findings, risk dashboard, and remediation roadmap; active for 90 days
- **PDF report** — a client-ready document covering every finding, its severity, and the evidence supporting it, with an AI-generated executive summary in plain language
- **Remediation roadmap** — a phased action plan (0–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days) that tells you what to fix first and why
- **30-day check-in** — a follow-up re-scan at 30 days showing measurable progress on quick-win items

---

**Who it's for**

Mid-market organizations (25–500 employees) using Microsoft 365 in regulated or regulated-adjacent industries — community banking, healthcare, law firms, and government contractors — that are using AI tools without formal governance and need a defensible record of their posture.
