## Outreach Notes

**What NOT to include in cold outreach:**
- Vault, trust binding, or cryptographic signing — this is internal implementation, not buyer language
- CI/CD, test gates, or engineering metrics
- Technical architecture details
- "We are building the platform that..." — leads with product, not prospect pain

**What ALWAYS to include:**
- Specific industry context (community bank, medical group, law firm) — it shows you know their world
- Microsoft 365 / Entra ID mention — it's the technical qualifier that separates your addressable market
- Concrete deliverables (written report, portal, remediation roadmap) — not vague "insights"
- Price transparency — builds trust and qualifies faster

**Follow-up rule:** One follow-up per outreach attempt. After two touches with no response, stop. If they respond negatively, stop immediately and thank them for the clarity.
